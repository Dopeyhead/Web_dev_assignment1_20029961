Frequently Asked Quesetions

Here are some questions that are frequently asked about learning Japanese, as well as things I was curious about when I started learning.

Is Japanese hard to learn?

Yes, Japanese is considered to be difficult to learn for an English speaker, especially when constrasted with learning other European languages that have more similarities to English. Japanese and Chinese are estimated to take two years for learn when studying hard and full time, whereas Spanish and French are estimated to take 6 months to a year. However, Japanese is not impossible to learn and many people have learned it successfully. It can also be rewarding and interesting to learn such a different language.

Is it useful to learn Japanese?

Again when compared to learning other European languages, Japanese does not offer than many job opportunities. However, if you are interested in the culture it can give you some insights. Similarly, if you would want to travel to Japan, it can be useful to know some Japanese. And may be the only way to communicate and connect with locals.

What is the best way to learn Japanese?

Traditionally, when people seek to learn a language, they take classes and learn in a classroom setting. However, there are many other ways to learn Japanese. One of the best ways is through language immersion. Where you absorb the language naturally. I believe that memorisation of phrases and vocabulary is most effective way and suggest using Anki a website for learning flashcards with spaced repetition. There are many online resources available for learning Japanese. Check out the resources page for some suggestions. Consistent pratice and a desire to learn are the most important factors in learning Japanese. So 頑張って- がんばって - Gambatte! - Do your best!

What are the biggest differences between Japanese and English?

Sentence structure and the writing systems are both quite different from English. You can read more about them in the alphabet and grammar pages. Things like dropping subjects in context and the absence of a future tense make Japanese feel very different. Finally, politeness rules are completely absent from English.

Is the Japanese language related to the Chinese language?

Chinese is Sino-Tibetan and Japanese is considered a language isolate. Kanji uses chinese characters and there are many Chinese loan words in Japanese. Korean actually mirrors Japanese structutally but both are offically considered isolates, they do not share a common ancestor. However there theorized that all three of these languages have had contact ove the years due to geographical closeness which results in 'lingustic convergence'.

Do I need to learn Kanji?

Kanji is a very difficult writing system to learn. However, it is very useful to know some Kanji as it is used in many words and can help you understand the meaning of words. Especially when travelling there will be some things only written in Japanese. Even for just learning how to speak, it is useful to know some Kanji as Japanese has many homophones and Kanji can help you understand the meaning of words. However, it is not necessary to learn Kanji to learn Japanese. You can learn to speak and understand Japanese without learning Kanji. It is up to you how much you want to learn and it is often better left for later.
